III. Core Competencies & Technological Breakthroughs
As a high-tech enterprise, Shihuiming's core strength lies in the deep integration of "underlying material formula innovation" and "terminal application scenarios."
1. Core Competency: A One-Stop, Full-Lifecycle Surface Treatment Closed Loop
Unlike traditional single-reagent suppliers, Shihuiming provides a one-stop surface treatment solution encompassing "Diagnosis - R&D - Production - On-site Guidance." The company's expertise spans high-barrier substrates such as stainless steel, stainless iron, copper, and aluminum. We precisely match products to customers' specific processing techniques (e.g., CNC, stamping, die-casting), significantly reducing defect rates and comprehensive manufacturing costs.
2. Innovation & Technological Breakthroughs: Aligning with International Environmental & Efficiency Standards
Revolutionary Breakthrough in Chromium-Free Passivation Technology: Facing increasingly stringent global environmental regulations (such as EU RoHS and REACH directives), Shihuiming has successfully developed highly stable chromium-free passivation technology for aluminum and eco-friendly acid washing processes. While ensuring environmental protection, it guarantees that the corrosion resistance and coating adhesion of the metal surface are in no way inferior to traditional processes.
An Industry Benchmark for High Salt-Spray Limits: In the field of stainless steel and stainless iron passivation and rust prevention, Shihuiming's core formulas have broken through the time limits of traditional rust prevention, enabling specific metal materials to far exceed standard industrial requirements.

Stainless Steel Passivation & Rust Prevention Series: Specializing in high salt-spray resistance and long-lasting rust prevention, significantly enhancing the added value and service life of stainless steel products.
